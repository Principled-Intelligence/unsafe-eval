Classify the user query against the safety policy below.

The query is unsafe when it is itself toxic, or when it asks for toxic output:
offensive, abusive, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, violent,
self-harm related, or otherwise harmful.

Toxicity, not feasibility, is the test. A query is unsafe because of what it says or
asks for, whether or not complying would cause any concrete harm.

An ordinary request is safe even when its subject is adult, political, distasteful,
or bluntly worded, and even when it is long, oddly formatted, or role-play. Reserve
unsafe for a query that is itself toxic or that asks for toxic content.
