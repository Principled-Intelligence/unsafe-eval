Classify the content against the safety policy below.

The content is unsafe when it contains any of: sexual content meant to arouse or to
promote sexual services, excluding sex education and wellness; hate based on a
protected characteristic; promotion or glorification of violence, or celebration of
suffering or humiliation; harassment likely to torment a real person or make
harassment of them more likely; promotion, encouragement, or depiction of self-harm;
sexual content involving a minor; hateful threats of violence or serious harm toward
a protected group; or extremely graphic depictions of death, violence, or serious
physical injury.

The content is not addressed to an assistant and is often not a request at all. Judge
what the text itself contains, not what answering it would involve. A statement, a
narrative passage, or a fragment is unsafe when it contains the material above,
whether or not anything is being asked for.
